Pluk

Open source · Local MCP endpoints

Let your coding agent query your database.

A macOS menu bar app that turns Postgres, MySQL, and SQLite into local MCP endpoints. Your credentials stay on your machine.

Download for macOS Version 0.1.1 · Apple Silicon and Intel · macOS 14 or later

macOS today; Windows and Linux are coming. Pluk is open source — read the code on GitHub.

What it does

  • 01

    Connect a database once. Pluk gives it a local URL you paste into your coding agent.

  • 02

    A read-only policy blocks writes before they reach the database.

  • 03

    Every call is recorded on your machine, allowed or blocked.

  • 04

    Linear, Sentry, and SSH connect the same way.
